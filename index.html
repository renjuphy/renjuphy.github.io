About

Assistant Professor at the Department of Physics, Government College Kasaragod, with expertise in computational chemistry and materials science. My research focuses on predicting material properties through advanced computational techniques, developing functional materials for nonlinear optical and optoelectronic applications, and modeling two-dimensional nanomaterials for biosensing applications.
